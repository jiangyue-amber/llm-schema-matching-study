Identifier for a single measurement type in the database. Each row associated with one ITEMID (e.g. 212) corresponds to an instantiation of the same measurement (e.g. heart rate). Metavision ITEMID values are all above 220000. A subset of commonly used medications in CareVue data have ITEMID values between 30000-39999. The remaining input/output ITEMID values are between 40000-49999.
